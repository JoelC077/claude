Generated by rr-game-feel from `feel.json`; do not edit, regenerate (`feel.py spec`). Times are seconds from the event; motion channels run on the feel clock and hold during hit-stop; flashes, haptics, hit-stop and cues run on real time. Phone pixels use `tech.ui_platform.phone` 844 x 390 and `tech.camera.fov_v` 70.

Tiers: 1 fail, 2 crisis, 3 commit, 4 reward, 5 ui. A lower tier never outshouts a higher one (validate checks loudness).

## Sustained shake (trauma floor)
- Speed: floor = 0.22 x Speed / 50 (`av.vfx.speed_link`); at top speed 1.66 px.
- Pressure: floor = 0.22 x clamp((p - 0.8) / 0.20); at redline 1.66 px (numbers: OQ-013 default).
- Cap 0.29: both at once 2.89 px; reduce motion: 0.

## Lever drag
- Knob = detent x (finger / detent)^1.6 until the finger passes 70% of the travel (heavy), then lever_commit; the knob snaps home Back Out 0.18 s. Released early: lever_snapback and a Back Out 0.25 s return. World handle throw +-35 deg. Input method: OQ-031 (default A, drag).
