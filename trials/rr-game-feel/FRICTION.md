## What worked
- `validate --strict` caught the one real design error at once, with a clear message: the first hard_brake
  (loudness 0.276) outshouted every tier-2 crisis.
- The mission copy (`RR_FEEL_PRESETS`) kept the skill untouched. `list` and `show` give measured numbers per event.
  `plot all` takes 0.8 s, a group preview about 10 s with GIFs, and build + luaparse + bible check 1.2 s.
- `luatest.py --presets` ran the Lua runtime on the modified presets (34 events including the new hard_brake, full
  and reduce motion) in 1 s, and camera/FOV matched the preview frame by frame.
- Canon-first reads were precise and cheap: brake formula, OQ-018 cord, the crisis_extra numbers.
- `crit` plus `critic_kit build` produce self-contained critic orders of about 3k tokens each.
