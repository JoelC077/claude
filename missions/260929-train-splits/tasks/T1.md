Cutting (exactly what Studio CSG will do): FRONT cutter = union of boxes per cell {X cell, Y cell, Z in [-zext, d]}; REAR cutter = {X cell, Y cell, Z in [d, +zext]} (B coords). front piece = mesh minus REAR cutter; rear piece = mesh minus FRONT cutter. Weld the OBJ group mesh first (merge_vertices), use manifold3d booleans; if a mesh is not watertight, fall back to clipping its triangles per cell (no caps) and say which crossers used the fallback. Cap faces get the crosser's own material (Roblox does that). The two plain Blocks (Part269, Part409) are sliced per cell (boxes).
Motion (break_spec motion/topple): V = 35, a = 12. drift(t) = 0.8*(1-cos(pi*min(t/0.2,1)))/2 + (0.5*a*t^2 if t <= V/a else 0.5*a*(V/a)^2 + V*(t - V/a)) along +Z. Topple of each lost body about the pivot line parallel to Z at (X = +-11.7, Y = -8.6) in its own carriage's B frame, on the falling side: roll = 88*u^2 over 1.05 s (u = (t - delay)/1.05, delay 0.45) then 88 -> 82 -> 88 over 0.35 s (QuadOut/QuadIn halves); yaw 0 -> 7 deg about the body's vertical centre line over the roll; sink 0 -> 0.6 in the last 0.1 s of the roll. Break 1 has two bodies: body 1 = C1.Rear (incl. gangway), body 2 = C2 (both halves) with delay 0.8, roll 86 over 1.2, bounce 86->80->86 over 0.4, yaw -4, sink 0.5. Break 2: one body = C2.Rear (body-1 numbers). For the renders: break 1 falls toward +X, break 2 toward -X.
Scene: ground at rail level (world y 5.33) and a simple track under the train (two rails, sleepers) along Z so the derailment reads; same sun/world as scene.py.
States: intact; exploded (halves pulled 6 studs apart along Z, design view); break1 at t = 0.3, 1.0, 1.6, 4.0; break2 at t = 1.2, 4.0.
