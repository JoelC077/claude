# Torn carriages (carriage split)
Purpose: Joel's 2-carriage train tears in half at one designed break point per carriage. It must "look really good and accurate": no visible seam while intact; a jagged, believable tear when it snaps; the lost part derails, topples onto its side and falls behind with the scrolling terrain; the kept half's torn end reads as torn wreckage, not a clean slice.
Audience: Risky Rails players (Roblox, 13+ mixed); the owner as art director.
Player view: players never leave the train; the train stands still while the world scrolls; third-person camera ~9.5 studs above the floor, vertical FOV 70; players are inside the carriages, on the roofs and on the gangway. The kept half's torn end is seen close from inside (Cam_POV_In) and from the roof (Cam_Roof3P); the lost part is seen receding and toppling (Cam_Wide, Cam_Side); Cam_Game is the 400 px game-distance view.
Stage: draft v1 (geometry + motion). Explosion, smoke, embers and sound are separate deliverables judged on their own boards, so they are absent from these renders by design.
Fixed constraints: the carriages are the owner's model and stay as built except the cut; only the 10 long pieces crossing each break are cut (roof x4, wall shells x2, curtain pelmet, carpet, floor plate, ceiling strip); the tear is the stepped cell pattern from the break spec (walls within +-0.9 stud inside the window pillar, roof bite up to 4.8 forward, floor tongues up to 3.6 back); Roblox CSG gives cut faces the part's own material and colour; studs; exactly two carriages.
Owner worries / already decided: "make each split look really good and accurate"; builder unioned a lot (only crossers get cut, nothing separated by hand); no coupling hardware (owner: "dont bother with the coupling stuff"); big explosion and topple requested (effects judged separately); world scrolls, train never moves. step 2: pre-answered
