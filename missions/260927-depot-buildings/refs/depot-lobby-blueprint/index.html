General Notes

  • 01 — All dimensions in studs (1 stud ≈ 0.28 m). Grid square = 5 studs, labelled ruler = every 10.
  • 02 — Footprint sized for a 6-player server per the game plan — this is a small hub, not a large public plaza. Don't over-build it.
  • 03 — Treat every number here as a starting point, not gospel — nudge wall and zone positions once actual fence-panel and station-building footprints are measured in Studio.
  • 04 — Outer boundary of the main lobby and depot yard is the palisade fence itself (mix bent-bar / vine-overgrown panels along the run) — there's no separate architectural wall.
  • 05 — North (↑) = direction of travel from spawn to the join-queue platform.

Floor Paving Plan

Scale  1 square = 5 studs

The rule: keep the ground clean along the routes players actually use — the centre path, the queue platform, the run up to spawn — so the space reads clearly. Push the cracking, lifted slabs and grass straight to the fence line and yard corners, where nobody's walking and decay reads as atmosphere rather than an obstacle.

Paving Key

Clean walkway — path, platform, spawn approach
Weathered concrete — everyday yard slab
Cracked / lifted slab — edges & corners only
Grass through the seams — fence line & corners
Gravel ballast — under the display track
