# Depot Lobby buildings: Depot + Main Hall
Purpose: the two buildings of the Risky Rails Depot Lobby (walk-around hub, 6 players): the Depot station house (24x14 studs, two-gable stone, front faces north onto the yard/display track) and the Main Hall (28x12 anchor building north of the join-queue platform, facing south to spawn). Score each building separately (per-building score block), then overall = lowest.
Audience: Risky Rails players (Roblox, 13+ mixed), owner as art director.
Player view: third-person eye 9.5 studs, vertical FOV 70, walking around a static lobby; seen mostly from 20-60 studs; 400x225 game-distance render included.
Stage: draft (v1 for pass 1).
Fixed constraints: studs (1 BU = 1 stud), 5-stud avatars, doors >= 7x9; <= 10k tris per part, <= 20k per building; one 256px palette atlas (32px cells), one material; every recolourable part a separate named object; weathered-rural railway style (stone, iron, timber, moss, soot, brass), decay at edges, clean routes; chunky readable forms.
Owner worries / already decided: facade-only (open doorways, no interiors); Main Hall placement/size is an assumption (not on blueprint); no invented signage text (name board blank placeholder); owner wants many separate parts for recolouring. step 2: pre-answered
