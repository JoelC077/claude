# P5 design: VFX + animation v2 (split at 70%, split at 30%, full-train explosion at 0%)
Goal: a buildable design for the look and motion of all three integrity events. It must work on any train, be safe on
phones, and aim at 9/10 on the fx critic (v1 got 5/10).

Read first: M/refs/context.md, M/refs/v1-kit.facts.md, M/refs/canon-skills.facts.md and
M/refs/roblox-vfx-physics.facts.md. Skim SK/rr-vfx-lighting/SKILL.md only for its preset schema and gates.

Write M/design/vfx-animation.md (max ~3,000 tokens) with these sections:

1. Diagnosis: why v1's fx scored 5/10 (from the v1 facts), and the 5 changes that fix it.

2. Beat sheets. One per event (70% C2 split, 30% C1-and-rest split, 0% full train), from -3 s to +8 s.
   Columns: t | beat | VFX layers (emitters, meshes, lights, post) | motion | camera | sound cue id | CORE or PROPOSED.
   Sound cue ids are names only; the sound designer owns the sounds.
   Cover these beats:
   - the pre-snap warning phase (PROPOSED unless Joel asked for it)
   - the snap, the explosion and the glass
   - debris: real flung parts or particles; decide, with counts
   - torn-edge smoke and fire
   - wreck motion versus terrain drift, and the topple
   - the wreck landing: secondary explosion (PROPOSED)
   - the 0% chain reaction across whatever is left (locomotive, tender, carriages)
   - the hand-off to game over

3. Animation approach. For each of (a) the topple, (b) debris and (c) the full-train blow-up, pick one of scripted,
   physics or hybrid. Give your reasons (smoothness on phones, server cost, replication, determinism, works on any
   train) and a fallback.

4. Train-agnostic rules: how the same events work on the CoalTrain, the Diesel and future trains.
   - Per-train flavour as config. Coal: boiler, steam, embers. Diesel: fuel fireball, black smoke.
   - Chunking the full train for the 0% blow-up from labels or bounding boxes.
   - No per-train hand-placement beyond one config block.

5. Budgets per event on a mid-range phone:
   - particles alive, emitters, lights, moving bodies
   - server CFrame writes per frame
   - fallbacks by graphics quality
   - flash-safety and reduce-motion rules

6. Preview plan:
   - how this cloud session shows Joel the look before Studio: renders or GIFs, using the real particle textures where
     possible
   - how the critic judges it: profile F plus a motion check

7. Build task list. For each task: deliverable | specialist skill and commands | depends on | model/effort |
   est. tokens | done-when.

Reply in at most 10 lines: the path, the 3 key decisions and the 3 biggest risks.
