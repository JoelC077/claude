# P4 research: Roblox audio, licence-clean sourcing and production for "literally the best sounds ever"
Goal: the facts, and the honest production path, for top-tier destruction audio in Roblox, including what can and cannot
happen in this cloud session.

Read first: M/refs/context.md. Then read V1/export/sounds/SOUNDS.md and V1/critique-sound/ledger.md to see where v1
stood.
Tools: load WebSearch and WebFetch with ToolSearch ("select:WebSearch,WebFetch"). The shell cannot reach roblox.com,
freesound.org or sonniss.com because of the network policy.

A. The Roblox audio engine (Oct 2026). Give a source URL, date and confidence for each item.
- The Audio API: AudioPlayer, AudioEmitter, AudioListener, AudioDeviceOutput, Wire, AudioFader, AudioEqualizer,
  AudioCompressor (sidechain?), AudioReverb, AudioEcho, AudioDistortion, AudioPitchShifter, AudioFilter, AudioLimiter,
  AudioChorus/Flanger, AudioAnalyzer, and any time-stretch.
- Distance attenuation curves, and occlusion or acoustic simulation features.

- The maximum number of voices, and performance on phones.

Flag anything that is NOT allowed (NC, personal-only).

D. Production recipe for AAA-feeling destruction audio (concise; cite sources where you can)
- Layer anatomy for each event type:
  - metal stress and creak; tear and rip; glass
  - explosion (close, mid, far); debris rain; heavy impact or crash; scrape and drag
  - fire or burning loop; sirens or alarms, if any
  - ragdoll whoosh and impacts; comedic sweeteners
- Variation: round-robin, pitch and gain jitter.
- Perspective and distance versions.
- Silence or a duck just before the boom.
- Low end that survives phone speakers: harmonic saturation; the phone band is roughly 300 Hz-8 kHz.
- Loudness targets for games: short-term and integrated LUFS, true peak.
- Pitching down for slow-mo.
- Priority and voice stealing.
