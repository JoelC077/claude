# P6 design: sound v2 ("literally the best sounds ever")
Goal: a sound design and production plan that can honestly reach top-tier quality for the three integrity events and the
clip moments, given what this cloud session can and cannot do.

Read first: M/refs/context.md, M/refs/v1-kit.facts.md, M/refs/canon-skills.facts.md and M/refs/roblox-audio.facts.md.
Skim SK/rr-soundsmith/SKILL.md for its map and mix schema, recipes, measure and fix tools, and licence gate.

Write M/design/sound.md (max ~3,000 tokens) with these sections:

1. Where v1 stands (synthesised placeholders? scores?) and the quality bar for v2. Define "best ever" in checkable terms:
   - layers, variations and perspective
   - the loudness ladder and translation to phone speakers
   - timing to the frame
   - no repetition fatigue
   - Joel signs off after listening

2. Sound event map v2.
   Columns: event id | trigger (integrity threshold or beat time) | layers (transient, body, low end, tail, sweeteners) |
   variations | 3D emitter or 2D | perspective versions | duck/priority | CORE or PROPOSED.
   Cover:
   - warning creaks and alarms
   - tear, glass, explosion (close, mid, far)
   - debris rain, topple crash, scrape and drag, fire loop
   - the wreck landing
   - the 0% chain reaction, the final blast and the game-over sting
   - ragdoll fling whoosh and impacts, and other clip moments: comedic sweeteners within the canon tone

3. Engine architecture:
   - Audio API or legacy: decide, with reasons and fallbacks
   - the bus and mix graph: ambience, train, events, UI, music
   - ducking and sidechain, distance curves, slow-mo pitch handling
   - the preload and latency plan, and the voice budget on phones

4. Sourcing and production pipeline:
   - a source for each layer: licence-clean only; name the packs and search terms
   - who fetches: the owner or the cloud session, given the network policy
   - the processing chain, using tools that install here
   - mastering targets, naming and versioning
   - how files reach Roblox (Joel uploads; the ids go into config)
   - the licence ledger

5. Quality gates:
   - soundsmith measurements
   - the Profile S critic. Be explicit that critics cannot hear, and say what they check instead.
   - a structured listening test by Joel: phone speaker and headphones, A/B variants, pass rules

6. Build task list. For each task: deliverable | skill and commands | depends on | model/effort | est. tokens |
   done-when | owner steps.

Reply in at most 10 lines: the path, the 3 key decisions and what Joel must do.
