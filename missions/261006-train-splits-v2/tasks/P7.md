# P7 design: full-train explosion (game over) + clipability
Goal: design the 0% integrity game-over sequence and the "clipability" layer (players getting flung, other funny
moments) so that both are fun, readable, safe and buildable. Score each idea for value against cost.

Read first: M/refs/context.md, M/refs/v1-kit.facts.md, M/refs/canon-skills.facts.md and
M/refs/roblox-vfx-physics.facts.md.

Write M/design/clip-gameover.md (max ~3,000 tokens) with these sections:

1. Each integrity event as seen by three players: one on the lost section, one on the kept section near the break, and
   one far from it. Second by second, at a high level, cover what each sees, hears and feels, and what they can do. The
   VFX designer owns the beat sheet.

2. Fling system:
   - who gets flung: players on lost sections, and players near the blast
   - how: ragdoll plus impulse (direction and size ranges), spin, maximum air time, landing, and get-up or respawn rules
     tied to the game's rules
   - seated players, and players already in the air
   - server authority with client execution (network ownership)
   - anti-grief: no player can fling another
   - motion comfort: camera, duration caps, reduce-motion

3. Clip moments catalogue: 8-12 ideas. Examples:
   - slow-mo snap for nearby players
   - a callout of how far a player flew
   - ragdoll cam
   - flying props: luggage, coal, seats
   - the final blast launching everyone
   - a capture/share prompt via CaptureService
   - comedic landings
   Table columns: idea | what it looks like | why it clips | cost (S/M/L) | risk | canon/tone fit |
   CORE (owner asked: flinging) or PROPOSED | needs mechanic review?

4. The 0% full-train explosion as a game-over beat:
   - the build-up below about 10% integrity (PROPOSED)
   - the order of the chain reaction, with everyone flung
   - the camera: cinematic wide shot, slow-mo
   - the time until the game-over screen
   - how it fits Joel's existing game-over or round flow. This is unknown: list exactly what we need from his code.
   - works on any train: Coal, Diesel, future trains

5. Recommendation: the MVP clip set for v2 (at most 5) and a later set, with reasons.

6. Build task list. For each task: deliverable | skill and commands | depends on | model/effort | est. tokens |
   done-when.

Reply in at most 10 lines: the path, the MVP clip set and the 3 biggest risks.
