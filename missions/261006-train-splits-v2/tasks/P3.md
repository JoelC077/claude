# P3 research: Roblox engine facts for VFX, animation, ragdoll fling and clips (as of Oct 2026)
Goal: verified, current engine facts the v2 designers can build on, each with a source and a confidence label.

Read first: M/refs/context.md.
Tools: load WebSearch and WebFetch with ToolSearch (query "select:WebSearch,WebFetch"). The shell cannot reach
roblox.com because of the network policy, so use these tools. Prefer the create.roblox.com docs and Roblox release notes
or DevForum announcements from 2024-2026, and note the dates.

Give every answer as: fact | how it helps or limits v2 | source URL | date | confidence (verified in docs / announced /
community / unverified).

A. VFX
- ParticleEmitter:
  - flipbooks: layouts, modes, frame limits and texture size limits
  - Squash, Brightness, LightEmission/LightInfluence
  - Shape/ShapeStyle/ShapeInOut, Acceleration/Drag
  - TimeScale, LockedToPart, ZOffset, Orientation, WindAffectsDrag
- Beam, Trail and attachment-based emitters.
- Lights: PointLight and SpotLight limits.
- The Explosion instance: its visual, plus BlastPressure and DestroyJointRadiusPercent.
- Highlight.
- Post-processing (Bloom, ColorCorrection, DepthOfField, Blur, SunRays) and its cost on mobile.
- Any new VFX features from 2025-2026.
- How the graphics quality level scales particles, and the mobile budgets for particles and overdraw.
- Custom texture upload limits: size and moderation.

B. Animation and physics, for a falling half-carriage and a full-train blow-up
- Scripted CFrame/TweenService versus unanchored assemblies with HingeConstraint or BallSocket.
- Network ownership (SetNetworkOwner) of unanchored server parts, and how smoothly they replicate.
- The cost of unanchoring a model of 200-1,000 parts.
- Welds (WeldConstraint) and assembly rules.
- What StreamingEnabled implies.
- Client-only visual debris and deterministic replays.

C. Character ragdoll and fling (R15, 2026)
- The Avatar Joint Upgrade (AnimationConstraint) and how it affects ragdoll scripts.
- Humanoid states (Physics, Ragdoll, FallingDown, PlatformStand), BreakJointsOnDeath and RequiresNeck.
- Who must apply the impulse, given that each character is network-owned by its client:
  ApplyImpulse/AssemblyLinearVelocity on the HumanoidRootPart, and getting up again afterwards.
- Seated players at the moment of the fling (Seat:Sit, SeatWeld, Humanoid.Sit).
- Known exploit and stability pitfalls, such as flinging other players and physics glitches.

D. Slow motion and camera
- Is there any global time scale? (Expected: no.) The standard fakes: ParticleEmitter.TimeScale, tween and animation
  speed, the camera.
- Camera shake practice.
- Reduce-motion support: is there a GuiService or UserGameSettings reduced-motion API?
- HapticService on phones and gamepads.

E. Clips and sharing
- CaptureService: screenshots, video capture APIs, prompts to share or save, and availability by platform and age.
- Roblox "Moments" or video clip features, and their status in 2026.
- Any rules about prompting players to capture.
- What an experience can do to make moments clippable (UI-free cinematic beats, kill-cam).
