## pillars · The pillars (what makes it Risky Rails)
- `identity.pillars.fork_bet` = `every junction is a physical lever bet: safe branch or risky branch with stacking multipliers and a hidden hazard` | the one mechanic competitors lack; show it in every thumbnail | src: PLAN, LPB | canon
- `identity.pillars.banked_fare` = `fare is banked at stations; a crash loses only what is not banked` | fixes Land or Die's all-or-nothing and Bumpy Flight's zero-progress runs | src: PLAN | canon
- `identity.pillars.physical_loud` = `interactions are physical and visible (lever, shovel, wrench), never a quiet menu or vote` | "a lever is a clip" | src: PLAN, MRS | canon
- `identity.pillars.funny_failure` = `failure is funny and chaotic, never punishing or arbitrary` | contrast: Land or Die's 22% success rate is a cautionary tale | src: PLAN, MRS | canon
- `identity.pillars.four_systems` = `four crisis systems, not eight: firebox/coal, boiler pressure, breakdowns, passengers` | a new mechanic that needs a 5th system is a red flag | src: PLAN, MRS | canon
- `identity.pillars.reuse_verbs` = `reuse interaction verbs (carry one thing in hand, one order computer, one asset pool)` | a new verb costs real solo-dev time | src: MRS, THS | canon
- `identity.pillars.stable_train` = `the train never moves; the world scrolls, so players stand on it with zero jitter` | src: PLAN | canon
- `identity.pillars.solo_viable` = `solo is a real (harder, slower) game; event rate scales with crew size` | src: PLAN, R2A | canon
